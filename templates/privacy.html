Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic profile information such as your name and email address. As you use the service, we store the expense entries you create — including amount, category, date, and description — along with anonymized usage signals that help us keep the app reliable. We do not collect bank credentials, card numbers, or any data we do not need to operate Spendly.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide core Spendly features: showing your expense history, summaries, and category breakdowns. We may use aggregated, de-identified usage patterns to improve the product and prioritize new features. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your expense entries for advertising or profiling.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in a secure database with encryption in transit and at rest. Access is restricted to authorized systems and personnel. You may request export or deletion of your data at any time from your account settings; deleted data is removed from active systems promptly and from backups within a reasonable retention window.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly relies on a small number of third-party providers for hosting, email delivery, and error monitoring. These providers process limited data strictly on our behalf and under contractual confidentiality obligations. We do not share your expense entries with advertisers, data brokers, or any party outside what is necessary to run the service.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, want to access or delete your data, or believe your privacy rights have been affected, please reach out to us at privacy@spendly.example. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and work with you to resolve any concerns.
